## Preconditions

Not recorded.

## Area

Instructors page

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Go to https://pupilica.com/.
2. Scroll to the bottom of the page.
3. Click "Eğitmenler" (instructors).
4. Verify that the Instructors page opens.
5. Scroll down and locate the instructor card for "Batuhan Özcan".

## Evidence

![](../../Turkish-Reports/pupilica-web-defects/images/egitmenler-sayfasi-egitmen-profilinde-linkedin-butonu-olmamasi.gif)

## Expected Result

Each instructor card should provide a clickable LinkedIn button that opens the corresponding instructor's profile.

## Actual Result

The card for "Batuhan Özcan" has no clickable LinkedIn profile button. Users cannot easily view his professional background, and the card is inconsistent with other instructor profiles.
